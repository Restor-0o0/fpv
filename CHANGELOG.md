# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here.
The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and the project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [1.0.0] — unreleased

First public release for Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge 21.1.244.

### Added

- **Modular aircraft** — frames (tiny whoop, cinewhoop, 5", 7", hexa 6", octo 8"),
  motors, propellers, batteries, flight controllers, cameras and video transmitters,
  assembled at a dedicated assembly station.
- **Fixed-wing aircraft** — delta wing, glider, twin-boom cargo, carrier, scout,
  long-range, interceptor and loitering-munition airframes.
- **Flight model** — acro, angle and horizon modes with per-frame mass and thrust
  curves, battery sag, radio failsafe, propwash and turbulence.
- **Radio control** — RC transmitters (standard and long range) with per-aircraft
  binding, FPV goggles, and line-of-sight flying without goggles.
- **Gamepad and USB radio support**, with arming on an AUX axis.
- **Racing** — start, finish and gate blocks, saved routes, lap timing, and personal
  and track-record ghosts.
- **Payloads** — drop charges, storage bays, dart guns and six warhead families
  (frag, HE, heavy, shaped, thermobaric, cluster).
- **Stations** — assembly, control, kamikaze and charging stations, plus a
  drone nest that deploys and recovers aircraft autonomously.
- **NPC drones** — hostile, patrol and interceptor drones.
- **Anti-drone layer** — a detector that drives redstone and a jammer that cuts links.
- **Replay system** — save, scrub and play back the last 30 seconds of a flight.
- English and Russian translations.
